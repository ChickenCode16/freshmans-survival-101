Tips and Tricks

Use one folder for originals and one for photocopies.

Check requirements before traveling to campus.

Photograph receipts and confirmation slips.

Ask for written instructions if there are many steps.

Read the Course Map

Syllabus

Use your syllabus to plan ahead and understand what each course expects.

Course Information

This section explains what the course covers and the main ideas or skills students will learn. It outlines key topics and expected learning outcomes.

Grades may include quizzes, activities, projects, and exams. Check the syllabus for each category's percentage and how the final grade is calculated.

The syllabus lists absence limits and rules for late submissions. Policies vary by instructor, so check your instructor's requirements.

Look for dates of major exams and submissions. Write deadlines in a planner and update it when your instructor announces changes.

Review the required books, software, and supplies before the course starts. Ask your instructor what is required before buying anything.

Tips and Tricks

Read the entire syllabus during the first week.

Add major deadlines to your calendar immediately.

Keep a copy where you can find it quickly.

Ask early if a requirement is unclear.

Common Mistakes

Never Reading It

You may miss grading, attendance, or assignment rules.

Missing Deadlines

Write dates down instead of expecting to remember them.

Assuming Every Course Is the Same

Check each instructor's expectations separately.
